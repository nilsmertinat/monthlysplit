Data you enter in the app

To calculate how household costs are split, you enter names, monthly incomes, expenses, intervals and notes. This information is stored only on your device, inside the app’s private storage. It is not sent to us or to anyone else, and we have no access to it.

When you delete the app, this data is deleted as well. If you back up your device (for example with iCloud Backup or on your computer), the backup may include the app’s data. Those backups are controlled by your device settings and Apple.

What the app does not do

  • No user account or sign-in.
  • No analytics, tracking or advertising.
  • No third-party services or SDKs.
  • No connection to our own servers. We do not run any.

App Store, purchases and crash reports

The app is distributed through Apple’s App Store. Apple processes data for downloads, updates and any purchases under its own responsibility. You can read Apple’s Privacy Policy. If you have agreed in your device settings to share analytics with app developers, Apple may provide us with anonymous usage and crash statistics. These do not identify you.

Your rights

Under the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) you have the right to access, rectification, erasure, restriction of processing, data portability and objection (Articles 15–21 GDPR). Because we do not store any personal data about you, we will usually not hold anything to disclose. You may also lodge a complaint with a data protection supervisory authority.

Changes

If we add features that process data differently, for example optional syncing via iCloud, we will update this policy before releasing them.

Diese Angaben werden ausschließlich auf deinem Gerät im privaten Speicher der App gespeichert. Sie werden weder an uns noch an Dritte übertragen, und wir haben keinen Zugriff darauf.

Wenn du die App löschst, werden diese Daten ebenfalls gelöscht. Sicherst du dein Gerät (zum Beispiel per iCloud-Backup oder am Computer), kann die Sicherung die Daten der App enthalten. Diese Sicherungen richten sich nach deinen Geräteeinstellungen und liegen in der Verantwortung von Apple.
